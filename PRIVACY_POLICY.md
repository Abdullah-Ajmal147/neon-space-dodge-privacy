# Privacy Policy — Neon Space Dodge

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Neon Space Dodge ("the Game") is an Android game published by BotCraft Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game uses and how.

## Information stored on your device
The Game saves your progress on your device only: high score, coins, the ships you own and have equipped, sound settings, and whether you have seen the first-run hint. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it.

## Accounts and personal information
The Game has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. We do not run our own servers for the Game and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## Advertising
The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner while you play, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to revive or double your coins.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as model, OS version and ad interactions), and may use cookies or similar technologies. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised. This data is collected by Google, not by us. See Google's policies:
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices.** Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the Game shows a consent form before loading ads, and you can change your choice later. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

## Purchases
The Game has no in-app purchases.

## Children
The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google under its own policies.

## Data sharing and security
We do not sell your data and have no personal data to share. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob), as described above. Your saved progress stays in the Game's private storage on your device.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
